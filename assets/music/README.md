# Music

`christmas.mp3` (2 min 34 s, loops) is an instrumental medley of two public-domain Christmas melodies:
"Jingle Bells" (James Lord Pierpont, 1857) and "We Wish You a Merry Christmas" (traditional English carol).

No recording was used: every note is synthesised by `make_music.py` (music box, soft keys, pad, bass and
sleigh bells), so the file is free to play on the site and the office TVs.

To use another track, put the MP3 here and change `MUSIC.src` in `config.js`. It has to be royalty-free or
licensed for playing in the offices. Leave `MUSIC.src` empty to turn the music off everywhere.
