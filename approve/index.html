Orange flags are automatic checks: phone numbers or emails, a BMS that looks made up, and wishes sent from the same device. One device, one BMS: a wish from a device that already sent one for another BMS goes straight to Not approved. A person always decides, and "Approve all" skips flagged wishes. Prize standings

The wishes still in the like race, most liked first: the top ones are next in line for a Most liked prize in the daily draw (Prize draw). Likes stop counting at 11:59 pm on 18 December.

Only real-looking likes are stored: one per BMS and one per device, never the sender's own wish, and none after the deadline. "Not eligible" means the wish's BMS is in prize_excluded or looks made up (not 4 to 7 digits, one digit repeated, or a straight run like 1234567). An orange row means most of its likes came from one connection: open "Who liked" before you announce winners.

    Team numbers

    What each admin decided, by the name they typed when they signed in. Reviewed counts every wish or message they approved, set aside or deleted; accepted the ones they approved (an edit also approves). Wait is how long things waited, on average, before they decided.

    Campaign switch

    Draw winners

    Draw the evening before: you can draw today and up to 4 days ahead, one day after another (on Friday, draw Saturday, Sunday and Monday). Approved winners show on the tree at 9 am Malaysia time on their day, and each one gets a Grab code that only their own phone can open.

    The plan

      A day with too few people carries its prizes to the next draw. No one wins twice: anyone drawn once (also when redrawn) is out of later draws. Most liked is the top of the Most liked list when you press Draw (at least 1 like; same likes, the one approved first).

      Grab codes

        Add codes

        Codes are kept locked in the database: only the winner's phone can open one, or an approver who presses Show code (logged with their name, at most 20 an hour). A code that was opened is never given to anyone else.

        When a winner can't open it

        The code opens on the phone they used for the wish or the likes. On a new phone or after clearing the browser, they can't open it. Check their BMS on WhatsApp, then use Show code and send it to them. It then counts as given, and your name is logged.

        Redraw replaces a winner with a new draw from the same pool, for example a leader or organizer, or a code not opened after 3 days. It needs a reason, and it isn't possible once the code was opened.
