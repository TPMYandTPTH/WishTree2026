The most-liked approved wishes. Only real-looking likes are stored: one per BMS and one per device, never the sender's own wish, and none after the deadline. "Not eligible" means the wish's BMS is in prize_excluded (Amr, Maszy, the Wish Tree team) or looks made up (not 4 to 7 digits, one digit repeated, or a straight run like 1234567).
